# P1.4 — does Simonetti's real biology break the antagonism? (the model can't say)

_Cope Labs / Seth · 2026-06-20 · model [tip_model_p14.py](tip_model_p14.py) +
[p14_check.py](p14_check.py) · follows the [P1.3 addendum](P1.3_findings.md)._

## Goal

Test the two Simonetti-grounded refinements the addendum identified, on the *stable*
P1.3-Part-1 model:
- **ν** — dual (TIP-producing) cells are partially immune-invisible (less WT/Env antigen
  + CTL-escape mutations): killed at ν·kill, contribute ν to CD8-priming antigen.
- **A_def** — a persistent defective-clone antigen reservoir (Simonetti: ~10⁶–10⁷ cells,
  "1 in 3 infected") that sustains CD8 *independent* of active WT replication.

Question: does either/both open an immune-compatible regime (TIP lowers active WT while
CD8 stays primed)?

## What happened — apparent escape, then the audit gate killed it

The (ν, A_def) sweep at ψ=22 *looked* decisive: **38/169 immune-compatible cells**, best
ν=0.42 / A_def=2.2× active → active WT down **2.08 log** with CD8 **94% retained**,
reachable at realistic reservoir sizes (≥0.5× active). Lever isolation even looked
mechanistic (needs *both* ν<1 and A_def; neither alone).

**It is an artifact.** The convergence check (`p14_check.py`) shows the escape cells
**oscillate** — Vw swings 80–600× over time, never settling — while only the
no-refinement case (ν=1, A_def=0, the P1.3 baseline) is stable (tail max/min = 1.000).
The ψ=22 endpoints fed to the metric were transient snapshots. The graded killing (ν<1)
**removes the stabilizing immune pressure on the TIP's factories and re-destabilizes the
ODE** — the same failure mode as the P1.3 spatial model. **The "escape" does not exist as
a fixed point; I cannot conclude the antagonism breaks.**

Within what I tried, escape and stability stayed coupled: the escape *requires* ν<1 (so the
TIP can establish), and every ν<1 case I ran oscillates. (Re-audit caveat: a *stable* ν<1
point **does** exist at very low immune gain — but the TIP is **inert** there (WTred = 0),
so it is not an escape. A stable *escape* was not reached; whether a better-designed,
non-oscillating immune model could reach one is untested — see [AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md) re-audit.)

## The real conclusion — the toy ODE has hit a hard wall

This is now the **third** realistic refinement to destabilize the model:
the dynamic immune feedback (P1.1, oscillated), spatial coupling (P1.3 Part 2,
oscillated), and graded immune-visibility + reservoir (P1.4, oscillated). The pattern is
unambiguous: **the antagonism is clean and stable ONLY in the simplest single-antigen-
pool, equal-visibility model. Every biologically-realistic ingredient reintroduces
oscillation that makes the endpoint metric meaningless.** The framework cannot decide
whether the antagonism survives real biology.

## The *mechanistic* read of Simonetti complicates the antagonism — both ways

Even though the model can't confirm it, the data changes the prior. The well-mixed
antagonism rests on one assumption — **a single antigen pool that the TIP fully
controls.** Simonetti shows that assumption is **false in reality**: large, persistent,
clonally-expanded defective-provirus populations present antigen **decoupled from active
WT replication** — precisely the CD8-sustaining source the well-mixed model lacks. That
mechanism *should* weaken or break the antagonism.

**Counter-caveat (also from the same data):** those persistent clones carry **CTL-escape
mutations** and make low Env — so the CD8 they sustain may be **escape-variant-specific,
not WT-controlling.** A reservoir that sustains the *wrong* CD8 specificity would
re-introduce a functional antagonism at the epitope level. The data cuts both ways.

## Revised bottom line for the whole arc

- **In the simplest model:** TIPs structurally antagonize CD8/vaccinal-effect control.
  Clean, stable, parameter-robust *within that model* (P1–P1.3, audited).
- **In reality:** genuinely **UNRESOLVED.** Two things keep it open, not "leaning against":
  (i) the multi-agent audit found the well-mixed antagonism is *partly by construction* —
  clearance is keyed to a single shared antigen pool, so "lower active infection → lower
  clearance driver" is semi-definitional; and (ii) Simonetti supplies mechanisms on *both*
  sides — the real reservoir decouples antigen from active replication (could break it),
  but its CD8 may be escape-variant-specific (could preserve it).
- **Tool verdict:** the practical end of *this* ODE approach — the oscillations may well be
  fixable with a purpose-built stable model, but not by another iteration of this one. A
  rigorous answer now requires a
  **stable-by-construction** model (stochastic / agent-based, or an immune model designed
  not to oscillate) — a separate project, with CLAWS-measurable reservoir/antigen
  parameters to anchor it.

The honest capstone: the model made a strong claim, the audit hardened it, real data
identified the assumption it hinges on and showed that assumption is false — and the
model proved unable to test its own failure mode. That's not a loss; it's the correct
boundary, found honestly, with the artifact caught (twice) before it became a claim.
