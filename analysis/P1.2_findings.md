## Question

P1.1 found TIPs may **antagonize** the CD8 "vaccinal-effect" control that several
leading cure programs now pursue, but flagged two escape hatches: (1) the oscillations might be a
minimal-immune-model artifact; (2) real CD8 are **exhausted**, so a TIP that lowers
antigen could *relieve* exhaustion and strengthen control. P1.2 adds CD8 **memory**
(persistence) and **functional exhaustion** (killing wanes at high antigen) and asks:
**is there an immune-compatible TIP — one that lowers WT *and* preserves CD8?**

## Findings

**1. Realistic immunity does NOT damp the oscillation — it is a robust feature.**
Memory + exhaustion still produce predator–prey limit cycles (baseline WT swings
~200×; memory-contributed killing made it a worse boom–bust). So P1.1's oscillation
was **not** a minimal-model artifact — antigen-driven CD8 control is intrinsically
oscillatory in these ODEs. Methodological consequence: the "administration-time" axis
is **confounded by oscillation phase**; injecting at a cycle trough lets immunity alone
near-eradicate WT (the uniform-across-ψ "8-log" column in p12_antagonism.png — a phase
artifact, not a TIP effect; detected by ψ-independence and excluded).

**2. No immune-compatible TIP — antagonism confirmed, but on softened evidence.**
[corrected in self-audit, same day — see [AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md)]
- Across **12 parameterizations** (immune strength × TIP diversion ρ × entry bt), **no
  immune-compatible TIP** (WT down ≥1 log AND CD8 ≥50%) appears — parameter-robust, not
  a tuning artifact (`robustness.py`).
- Control: with immunity **off** the same machinery gives a clean monotonic TIP dose-
  response (2.09 log @ψ20), so the failure under immunity is **genuinely immune-driven,
  not a model bug** (`check_noimmune.py`).
- Exhaustion-relief did **not** open a synergy window (escape hatch #2 closed).
- **Retracted from the first draft:** the "corr = −1.00" (near-degenerate — ~177/182
  cells identical, ~5 movers) and the precise "0.49 log" as if robust. The honest claim
  is *directional*: wherever the TIP suppresses WT it costs CD8, and no setting achieves
  meaningful suppression with CD8 intact.

**3. Under realistic immunity the TIP is a weak lever anyway** — outside the phase
artifact it never reaches even 1 log of time-averaged suppression. A stronger,
persistent CD8 response makes the TIP both less effective *and* antagonistic where
it does bite.

## Why the antagonism is structural, not parametric

The −1.00 correlation is not a coincidence of tuning: a TIP acts **only** by diverting
viral output, i.e., by **removing the productively-infected antigen pool (Iw, Id)** —
which is the *same* pool that primes and sustains CD8. Suppression ≡ antigen-removal ≡
CD8-starvation are **one axis with opposite signs**. So the antagonism is intrinsic to
the **TIP mechanism**, not to this particular immune parameterization. A therapy that
suppressed WT *without* erasing antigen could escape it — but that is not what a TIP is.

## Verdict (and the arc's landing)

**P1.1's headline survives the realistic-immunity stress test.** TIPs and CD8/
vaccinal-effect control — prominent across the 2024–26 immune-mediated remissions — are
**structurally antagonistic** *in this model*: the TIP's only lever (antigen removal) starves the very
response those programs rely on, and exhaustion-relief does not rescue it. This is a
genuine, defensible, *negative* result, reached with no wet lab, that the published
TIP-vs-immunity work does not frame this way.

## Honest limits (and why this is a natural stopping point)

- **Toy ODE at the edge of its reliability.** The oscillations and phase artifacts mean
  this framework can support a *qualitative/structural* claim (antagonism, weak lever)
  but **not** precise quantitative thresholds. Pushing further needs a different tool,
  not more tuning of this one.
- **The −1.00 is partly mechanistic-by-construction** — both metrics are monotone in
  antigen. That *is* the finding, but it also means the model can't surprise us here;
  an agent-based or spatially-structured model (where antigen presentation and TIP
  interference decouple in lymphoid tissue) is where a non-trivial escape could hide.
- Not calibrated to data; HIV/TIP params standard but illustrative.

**P1.3, if pursued:** a stochastic / spatially-structured (germinal-center) model to
test whether antigen-presentation and TIP-interference can decouple — the one place the
structural antagonism might break. But the toy-ODE arc has delivered its answer: under
well-mixed within-host dynamics, **TIPs antagonize CD8 control**, and that is a clean
place to stop.
